This research progress tracker is for master's and PhD students, and anyone running a long research project, who want to see each week whether the work is moving. It has three parts to copy: a weekly log, a monthly review for you and your supervisor, and a thesis timeline with milestones. Keep all three in one document.

## Part 1: the weekly log (15 minutes, every Friday)

Copy this block for each week. Write in short phrases. The point is the record of decisions and blockers, not prose.

```text
WEEK OF [date]                      Hours on research: [n]

DONE
- [task] -> [output: file, pages, records screened]
- [task] -> [output]

DECIDED (and why)
- [decision, e.g. dropped wave 3 of the survey] because [reason]

BLOCKED
- [blocker] | needs: [person or resource] | since: [date]

WINS
- [anything that worked, however small]

NEXT WEEK: THREE ACTIONS
1. [action with a visible output]
2. [action]
3. [action]

FILES TOUCHED
- [data or script name, version]
```

The "Decided" section is the one you will thank yourself for. When an examiner asks why you changed the sample, the answer and the date are in the log.

## Part 2: the monthly review (30 minutes, send to your supervisor)

```text
MONTH: [month year]          Timeline status: [on track / 2 weeks behind / ahead]

MILESTONES THIS MONTH
| Milestone            | Planned date | Status      | Note            |
| [e.g. ethics form]   | [date]       | [done/late] | [why, if late]  |

WINS (3 at most)
- [result, draft section, accepted abstract]

BLOCKERS I NEED HELP WITH
- [blocker] -> [the specific help, e.g. a signature, an introduction]

WHAT I WILL DELIVER NEXT MONTH
- [deliverable] by [date]

QUESTIONS FOR OUR NEXT MEETING
1. [question needing a decision]
```

Send it a few days before your supervisor meeting. A meeting that starts from a written review spends its time on decisions. If you need to ask for that meeting, the [research emails](/templates/research-emails) page has a template.

## Part 3: thesis timeline templates with milestones

A timeline turns the weekly log into a yes-or-no question: are you on track? Use one row per phase and one milestone you can show someone.

### A 12-month master's thesis timeline

| Phase | Months | Main tasks | Milestone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question and proposal | 1 to 2 | Topic, research question, short literature search | Proposal approved by supervisor |
| Ethics and setup | 2 to 3 | Ethics application, instruments, pilot | Ethics approval letter |
| Literature review | 2 to 4 | Search, reading, review chapter draft | Literature review draft |
| Data collection | 4 to 6 | Recruitment, surveys or interviews, cleaning | Clean dataset |
| Analysis | 6 to 8 | Analysis, tables, figures | Results chapter draft |
| Writing | 8 to 10 | Introduction, discussion, conclusion | Full first draft |
| Revision and submission | 10 to 12 | Supervisor feedback, editing, formatting | Thesis submitted |

### A 36-month PhD timeline

| Phase | Months | Main tasks | Milestone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations | 1 to 6 | Literature review, theoretical framework, methods training | Confirmation or qualifying report |
| Study 1 | 6 to 15 | Ethics, data collection, analysis | Study 1 paper submitted or chapter drafted |
| Study 2 | 13 to 24 | Design from Study 1 results, data, analysis | Study 2 chapter drafted |
| Study 3 or synthesis | 22 to 30 | Final study or integration across studies | Conference presentation |
| Writing up | 28 to 34 | Introduction, discussion, linking chapters | Full thesis draft |
| Submission and defense | 34 to 36 | Revisions, submission, defense preparation | Defense |

Both tables overlap phases on purpose: reading continues while you collect data. Hold back 10 to 20% of the total time as a buffer, and mark fixed dates you cannot move, such as ethics committee meetings, fieldwork seasons and the submission deadline. For how long each chapter should be, see [how to write a thesis outline](/blog/how-to-write-a-thesis-outline).

## What this research progress tracker and the tool do not do

- There are no Gantt charts, and the Progress tab does not fill itself in: you write each entry.
<!-- VERIFY: whether the Progress tab sends reminders or notifications when an entry or comment is added; the build spec does not say -->
- The AI summary uses only your entries and your supervisor's comments. It does not read your drafts or judge whether you are on track.
- The Research timeline does not know your program's rules, funding end date or your supervisor's leave. Add them as fixed dates, then check the plan with your supervisor.
- No tracker writes the thesis. It shows you, early, when the plan and the work have drifted apart.
