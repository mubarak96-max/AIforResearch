A research presentation maker turns your research into slides you can present. AI for Research's Slides builds a deck from a topic, a pasted paper or thesis chapter, or a Deep Research report: short bullets, speaker notes, citations on the findings and a references slide, as a PowerPoint file for defenses, talks, journal clubs and lectures.

## What you can do with it

- **Turn a Deep Research report into a talk.** Under a finished report, press Make slides in the "Build on this" row; the deck uses the report and its sources.
- **Turn your own paper or chapter into slides.** Paste it or upload it, and the deck follows your text.
- **Build a talk on a topic from the literature.** Leave Content empty and the deck is built from a search of the sources you choose, with filters.
- **Prepare a thesis defense** with the Thesis defence preset, then rehearse with the speaker notes.
- **Download for PowerPoint** and finish the design there.

## The options

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Topic or title | Required: the title of the talk, up to 400 characters |
| Content | Optional: paste a paper, chapter or notes, or upload a PDF, Word, text or Markdown file |
| Kind of talk | Research talk, Thesis defence, Conference presentation (12 to 15 minutes), Journal club, Lecture |
| Slides | About 8, 12, 16 or 20 |
| Sources and filters | When Content is empty: All papers, Medical, Preprints, Web, My Library, with year, study type and other filters |
| Your role | Pitches the language at your level, from undergraduate to academic staff |
| High quality | A stronger model, for 3 times the credits (Pro) |

Each preset shapes the order of the deck. A research talk runs motivation, question, approach, evidence and implications. A journal club covers the paper's question, methods, results, critique, take-home messages and discussion questions. A lecture builds from learning outcomes through concepts and examples to a summary and review questions.

## How it works, step by step

1. **Open Slides**, or press Make slides under a finished Deep Research report.
2. **Type the topic or title** of your talk.
3. **Add your content** if the deck should follow your own paper or chapter. Paste it, or press upload and choose a file.
4. **Choose the kind of talk** and the number of slides.
5. **Choose sources and filters** if you left Content empty, so the deck is built from the literature.
6. **Press Run.** The cost shows next to the button before you start.
7. **Read the deck in the app**: each slide shows its title, bullets with clickable citations, and its speaker notes. A slide with a table or chart says so.
8. **Press Download PowerPoint.** The deck is saved in your History, so you can download it again later.

## What the deck contains

- **A title slide** with the title and a subtitle.
- **Content slides**, each with a title and at most five short bullets of under 15 words.
- **Speaker notes** on every slide: what to say, about 60 to 120 words.
- **A table or a bar chart** on some slides, only where the content has real numbers to show.
- **Citations** at the end of bullets that state findings: clickable in the app, and written as (Author et al., Year) in PowerPoint.
- **A References slide** listing up to 14 cited sources, with DOI links.
- **Slide numbers** and a plain widescreen design with serif headings.

## What it does not do

Slides is a research presentation maker for content, not layout:

- **It is not a design tool.** Every deck uses one plain design. There are no themes, images or photos, and charts are bar charts only. Add your own figures and your institution's template in PowerPoint.
- **You cannot edit slides in the app.** Change the wording in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides after you download.
- **There is no PDF download.** Export the PowerPoint file to PDF from your presentation program.
- **A deck built from your own content cites no new papers.** It follows the text you gave it; a literature search runs only when Content is empty.
- **Check every number.** Charts and tables are made only from numbers in the content, but a model writes the slides: compare each figure with your thesis or paper before you present it.
- **The free plan cannot download.** It shows five slides so you can judge the deck; the full deck and PowerPoint file are on paid plans.
