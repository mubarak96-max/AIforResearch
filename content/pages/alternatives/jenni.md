If you like Jenni's editor but need the research and review steps around it, this Jenni AI alternative puts them in one account. AI for Research has an editor with cited autocomplete, and adds Deep Research reports, filtered paper search, a Reviewer that scores your draft and Thesis Suite tools for planning a thesis.

## What Jenni is best at

Jenni is a focused academic writing editor. Its autocomplete suggests sentences grounded in real papers and can draw only from a library you curate; you add sources by uploading PDFs, importing from Zotero or Mendeley, or using its search, which it says covers 200M+ papers [1]. Its AI chat reads your library and answers with citations, it formats references in 10,000+ styles, and it exports to .docx, LaTeX or HTML [1]. The free plan gives 10 autocompletes a day and 10 PDF uploads; Plus at $12 a month and Pro at $29 a month raise or remove the limits [2]. Its limits are simple counts of autocompletes, edits and chats rather than credits [2]. For a student who wants a clean place to write with citations and nothing else in the way, Jenni is a good fit.

## Where AI for Research differs

Both products give you a cited editor. AI for Research is wider: it also covers what comes before and after the draft.

- **Research before the draft.** Ask a question in the Ask box and get a cited answer (Summary, 1 credit) or a 2,500 to 4,000-word report after about 250 papers are screened and 12 full texts read (Deep, 15 credits). Open the result in the editor with live citations.
- **Paper search with filters.** Find Papers filters by year, study type, journal quality tier, citations, open access and peer-reviewed only, and leaves out retractions by default (0 credits).
- **A scored Reviewer.** Upload a thesis, proposal or manuscript, choose undergraduate, honours, master's or PhD level, and get each criterion scored out of 10 with comments that quote the passage and suggest a fix. Standard is 10 credits per 10,000 words; on the free plan, a preview of the first 3,000 words (the full scorecard and top 3 comments) costs 3 credits.
- **Thesis Suite.** Research question (FINER, PICO, PICOT, SPIDER), hypotheses, a thesis outline with a word budget, methodology, theoretical framework, research gaps, timeline and defence preparation, 3 credits each.
- **Checks on your claims and references.** Claim Check labels up to 10 claims as supported, partly supported, not supported or no evidence (2 credits). Citation check confirms each reference exists and flags retractions (0 credits).
- **An authorship report.** Share a record of how the document was written: typed, pasted and accepted from AI.

## How to use Jenni and AI for Research together

1. **Find the evidence here.** Ask your question in the Ask box, choose Deep, and set filters. Click Save cited papers.
2. **Export the references.** Add the key papers to a set in Citation Map and export it as RIS (0 credits).
3. **Write where you like.** Import them into Zotero and then into Jenni, or stay in the AI for Research editor and click Open in editor on the result.
4. **Review here.** Upload the finished draft to the Reviewer at your level and fix the quoted passages it flags.
5. **Check references.** Paste the reference list into Citation check before you submit.

## A worked example

*Example (invented, for illustration).* An honours student writing on urban heat islands drafts in a writing editor and has 14 sources. Before submission she uploads the 9,000-word thesis to the AI for Research Reviewer at honours level: 10 credits. The scorecard gives her literature review 6 out of 10, and two comments quote paragraphs that make claims with no source. She runs Claim Check on one paragraph (2 credits): two claims come back as supported, one as no evidence. She deletes that claim, runs Citation check (0 credits), and finds one reference with the wrong year. Total: 12 of her 45 free credits.

## What AI for Research does not do

- **Autocomplete is capped on every plan.** Free 10 a day, Plus 5,000 a month, Pro 20,000 a month; Team is unlimited.
- **No HTML export.** Word and PDF on every plan, LaTeX and PowerPoint on paid plans.
- **It does not write your thesis for you.** Drafts are starting points; the argument, checks and final wording are yours, within your institution's rules.
- **No AI-detector scores.** The AI writing checker shows stock phrases and patterns, not a probability.
- **Zotero and Mendeley import need a paid plan.**
