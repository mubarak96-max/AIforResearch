SAMPLE FUNDING CALL (fictional, for testing Proposal Studio)

University Research Fund: Early Career Research Grants 2027

Purpose
The Fund supports early career researchers (within seven years of their PhD or currently enrolled in a doctoral programme) to carry out a pilot study that can lead to a larger externally funded project. Priority areas for 2027 are student wellbeing, climate adaptation in cities, and digital learning.

Funding
Up to KES 1,500,000 for up to 18 months. Equipment may not exceed 25% of the budget. Overheads are not eligible.

What to submit (maximum 6 pages, excluding references and budget)
1. Specific aims (maximum 1 page): the problem, the aims and the expected results.
2. Background and significance: what is known, the gap and why it matters for Kenya.
3. Research design and methods: design, participants and sampling (with a sample size justification), measures, analysis plan and how threats to validity will be handled.
4. Ethics and data management: ethics approval route, consent, data storage and sharing.
5. Timeline: key milestones by quarter.
6. Budget and justification.
7. Pathway to impact and future funding: who will use the results and which external funder will be approached next.

Assessment criteria
Scientific quality (35%), feasibility (25%), relevance to the priority areas (20%), potential for future funding (20%).

Deadline: 31 March 2027.
