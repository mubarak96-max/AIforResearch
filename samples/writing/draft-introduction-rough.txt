Introduction (rough draft)

In todays world, cities are growing very fast and it is a very important issue that needs to be looked at. Nairobi is one of the fastest growing cities in Africa and there are many people moving there every year from rural areas. Because of this the green spaces in the city is being lost to buildings and roads and this could have a effect on the health of the people who lives there.

Mental health is also becoming a big problem especially for young people and students. Many students at universities in Kenya are having stress and depression and the counselling services can not cope with the number of students who need help. There are many reasons for this such as money problems, pressure from family, and not having good accommodation.

Research from other countries has shown that being in nature is good for mental health. For example a study by Ulrich in 1984 showed that patients who could see trees from their window got better faster. Other studies have shown that walking in nature makes people feel less stressed and think less negative thoughts. There is also a theory called attention restoration theory that says nature helps the brain to rest.

However most of this research was done in Europe and America and we dont know if it is the same in Africa where the green spaces are different and people use them in different ways. Some parks in Nairobi are not safe and some people can not get to them easily. This is why this study is needed.

The aim of this study is to find out if there is a link between green space and mental wellbeing among university students in Nairobi. This will help the university and the county government to make better decisions about protecting green spaces.
