# WriteGrid content plan: journalists, editors, script writers and non-academic researchers

30 articles to bring organic search and AI-answer traffic to WriteGrid from writers outside academia.

- **Keyword data:** Ubersuggest, United States (location 2840), pulled 5 November 2026.
- **Columns:** Vol = monthly US searches. SD = SEO difficulty, 0 to 100 (under 30 is easy, 30 to 45 is moderate, over 45 is hard). CPC = cost per click in US dollars, a rough sign of commercial value.
- **Length:** every article is at least 1,000 words. Most should be 1,400 to 2,200.

## 3. Writing rules for ranking in Google and in AI answers

1. **Answer first.** Put a 40 to 60 word direct answer to the title question right under the H1. AI assistants lift these blocks.
2. **One definition sentence per key term** ("Script coverage is …"). Use the same wording everywhere it appears.
3. **Tables and numbered steps.** Comparisons, cheat sheets and checklists get quoted far more often than prose.
4. **FAQ section with 4 to 6 questions** taken from the related keywords, marked up with `FAQPage` schema.
5. **Cite primary sources** (AP Stylebook, WGA, SPJ Code of Ethics, PRISMA, journal papers) and link to them. Content that cites sources gets cited.
6. **Show a dated byline and "last updated" date.** Revise yearly; titles below carry "2026" where freshness matters.
7. **Make the product section concrete.** Show a screenshot or GIF of the exact feature (claim-check comment, chat answer with page number, citation dropdown). Never just say "try WriteGrid".
8. **Add internal links** inside each cluster and to the cluster pillar (see section 5).
