# AI for Research messaging

The reference for all site and app copy. When you change a page, check it against this file first. Updated 2026-11-12 for the research-workspace direction (docs/redesign/01-product-structure.md).

## Audience

1. **Primary:** students and researchers doing research that has to stand on evidence: undergraduates, honours and master's students, PhD candidates (theses, systematic reviews, the defence), academic staff and supervisors, clinicians and medical researchers.
2. **Secondary:** industry and policy analysts who write research reports and briefs from papers, preprints and the web.
3. **Every field:** medicine and health, life sciences, data and computing, engineering, environmental studies, social sciences, humanities, business. Say "every field" with these examples; do not promise field-specific tools (field packs are phase 4).

Journalists, screenwriters and other writers are no longer a target on the home page or in the navigation. Their existing blog posts stay as they are.

## Core promise

**Research with real sources, from first question to defence.**

Short form (taglines, footer, meta): *The AI research assistant with real citations.*

Why: general AI chat invents references and cannot show where an answer comes from. AI for Research answers only from papers it found, shows how strong each source is, and carries the evidence into the next job (review, analysis, writing, slides) in one project.

## Pillars (in order)

| # | Pillar | Proof point | Product names |
|---|--------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Answers from real papers** | Every claim cites a paper you can open; markers the AI was not given are removed; a consensus line is counted from the studies | Deep Research (Summary, Deep, Detailed), Find Papers |
| 2 | **Evidence you can weigh** | Filters by year, study type, journal quality and citations; preprint and retraction flags on every paper | Find Papers, filters, Citation Map, Claim check |
| 3 | **From answer to finished work** | One click from a result to a literature review, thesis outline, gap analysis, slides or the editor with live citations | Writing Studio, Thesis Suite, Proposal Studio, Slides, the editor |
| 4 | **Checked before you submit** | Reviews at your level (undergraduate to PhD) with a scorecard; citations checked against Crossref and retraction data | Reviewer, Citation check, Systematic Review |

Supporting points (lists, not headlines): your PDFs, Zotero and Mendeley in one Library; Chat with PDF with page numbers; projects shared with a supervisor; Word, PDF, LaTeX and PowerPoint export; Data Lab statistics; works on a phone.

## Voice

Formal and friendly. Plain, direct words. (Unchanged.)

- Say what the feature does in one sentence. Lead with the verb ("Ask", "Find", "Check", "Write").
- Talk to the reader as "you". Talk about the product as "AI for Research", not "we", except in help and sign-in flows.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- No hype words: *revolutionary, supercharge, seamless, unlock, effortless, game-changing, cutting-edge*.
- Every price or limit names its unit: "5 credits", "30 credits a month", "25 PDFs".

## Not yet (don't put these in product copy)

- **Scopus or Google Scholar search.** Neither is searched. Scopus needs an institutional licence and Google Scholar has no API. Name only the sources above.
- **AI detection, or getting past detectors.** The writing pattern check highlights stock phrases and gives no score; the humanizer rewrites style. Never claim either makes text "undetectable".
- **Audience, field, alternatives and template landing pages** (`/for/…`, `/fields/…`, `/alternatives/…` except Elicit, `/templates/…`). They are planned in docs/redesign/04-content-plan.md, not built. Link to existing feature, tool and blog pages until each one exists.
- **Field packs** (gene, compound, patent, clinical-trial look-ups). Phase 4.
- **Team plan features** beyond "contact us": shared Library and pooled credits arrive with the Team plan.
- **Full text for every paper.** Many answers rest on abstracts; say "abstracts and open full texts".
- **AP style checker, screenplay formatting, website citation from a URL.** Unchanged from before: the editor does not do them.
