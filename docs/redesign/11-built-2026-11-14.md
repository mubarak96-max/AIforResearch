# What was built, 14 November 2026

Four features from the priority list: the PDF reader (2), evidence linked to the writing (1), project continuity (3) and meta-analysis (7a). Nothing is committed or deployed.

**State:** `tsc --noEmit` is clean and 718 tests pass across 77 files. `next build` was not run. **No page has been opened in a browser.** Test the reader, the editor's evidence card and the meta-analysis step in the emulators before launch.

## 1. PDF reader with highlights (`/library/[id]`)

- **Reading:**
  - pdf.js (`pdfjs-dist@5.4.624`) shows pages lazily, with a selectable text layer.
  - Zoom and fit-to-width, a page number box, and keyboard shortcuts (`+`, `-`, `0`, Esc).
  - The highlights panel becomes a bottom sheet on phones.
  - `?page=` opens at a page; `&highlight=` opens at a highlight, flashes it and selects it in the panel.
- **Highlights:** select text, then pick one of 4 colours or add a note. Highlights are private and stored at `library_documents/{id}/highlights/{hid}`.
  - Each one is checked against the PDF's extracted text and marked "not matched" if it isn't found there.
  - The match ignores case, punctuation and line-end hyphenation.
- **From a highlight:**
  - **Add to project:** it becomes a project finding awaiting review. The paper's project source is reused, or created if there isn't one.
  - **Add to matrix:** the highlight sets a matrix cell, with the quote and page as the cell's evidence. AI fills don't overwrite that cell.
  - **Cite this:** from the editor's Library panel (see 2).
- **Entry points:** **Read** on every PDF in the editor's Library panel, and **Open in reader** on a citation's evidence card.
- **API:** `GET/POST /api/library/documents/:id/highlights`, `PATCH/DELETE …/:hid`, `POST …/:hid/project`, `POST …/:hid/matrix`.
- **Costs:** no credits.

## 2. Evidence linked to the writing (editor)

- **Citations carry their evidence:** citations now also store the highlight, the project, the source, the finding, a fingerprint of the sentence (`claimHash`) and when it was checked (`checkedAt`).
- **Evidence card:** clicking a citation, then **Evidence**, shows:
  - the sentence
  - the passage and its page
  - the project finding's review status
  - links to the reader and to the finding
- **Checking:**
  - **The passage supports this sentence** records the sentence's fingerprint as checked.
  - Editing that sentence later gives the citation an orange wavy underline ("changed since checked") until it is checked again.
  - The fingerprint ignores case, spacing and punctuation.
- **Code:** `components/editor/lib/aiEditor/claimText.ts` (claim and fingerprint) and `claimCheck.ts` (the ProseMirror plugin, installed with the editor's other plugins).
- **`?block=<id>`:** opens a document at a paragraph.

## 3. Project continuity ("Needs another look")

- **What the editor reports:** about 5 seconds after an edit, the editor reports the citations that point at project evidence (`PUT /api/documents/:id/evidence-links`; document editors only, and only for projects where they can edit).
  - Links are stored at `projects/{p}/document_links/{documentId}`, so deleting a project removes them.
  - A citation removed from the document disappears from the project too.
- **What the project page shows** (`GET /api/projects/:id/attention`), at the top of the Work tab, for each document:
  - findings a reviewer sent back
  - sentences changed after they were checked
  - findings or sources that were removed
  - findings not reviewed yet

  Each item has **Open the sentence** and **Open the finding**, and the panel also shows the latest "next steps" and blockers from the progress log.
- **Privacy:** members who can't open a document see that something needs attention, but not its title or sentences. Links from deleted documents are removed.

## 4. Meta-analysis (Systematic Review, new step between Extraction and Report)

- **Effect sizes:** OR, RR, RD, MD, SMD (Hedges' g), and generic estimates.
- **Pooling:**
  - fixed effect: inverse variance or Mantel-Haenszel
  - random effects: DerSimonian-Laird or REML, with optional Hartung-Knapp
- **Results:** heterogeneity (Q, I², tau²) and a prediction interval, subgroups with a test for differences, leave-one-out, and the funnel plot with Egger's test (warning when there are fewer than 10 studies).
- **Plots:** forest and funnel plots export as SVG and PNG.
- **GRADE:** five downgrade domains and three upgrade domains with reasons. The certainty is computed; hints from the numbers are suggestions only. There is also a summary-of-findings table (CSV) and plain-English sentences to copy.
- **Storage:** `systematic_reviews/{id}/analyses/{aid}`, up to 30 outcomes, with rows capped at the plan's record limit. Saves are checked against the last revision (409 on a conflict) and logged in Decision history.
- **Validation:** checked against metafor's BCG example and an independent numpy/scipy script.
  - DerSimonian-Laird: −0.7141, SE 0.1787, tau² 0.3088, I² 92.12%, Q = 152.23.
  - REML: −0.7145, tau² 0.3132.
- **Costs:** runs in the browser, no credits.

## Known limits

- **Highlights:**
  - A highlight's "matched" flag is set when it is created; highlights made before the PDF's text was extracted stay "not matched".
  - A selection across two pages is saved on its first page.
- **Meta-analysis:**
  - "Fill from included studies" only offers extraction columns that hold numbers, so users add numeric columns in Extraction first.
  - The server doesn't check that a row's `record_id` belongs to the review.
- **Evidence checks:** only citations to project evidence are reported to the project. Citations of personal Library highlights still get the evidence card and the changed-sentence underline in the editor.
