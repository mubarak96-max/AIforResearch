Questions, filed

SourcesWhere do the sources come from?

From an index of 250M+ scholarly works (the OpenAlex catalogue), from PubMed when you choose Medical, from the web when you choose Web, and from the PDFs in your own Library. You pick the source before you ask.

CitationsCan it make up a citation?

Citations come from search results, not from the model's memory, so each one is a real record you can open. The sentences around a citation are still written by AI, so check that the paper says what the answer claims.

University rulesIs it allowed at my university?

Rules differ by university, course and journal. Check yours before you submit, and say how you used AI where they ask. Treat every draft as a starting point and rewrite it in your own words.

Free planWhat do I get for free?

30 credits a month for Summary and Deep answers, and a preview of each workspace. Paper search, citations, autocomplete and the Library do not use credits. No card is needed.

CreditsWhat is a credit?

Credits pay for the heavy work. A Summary answer costs 1, a Deep report 5, a Detailed report 20, a document review 10 and a slide deck 10.

Your LibraryCan I use my own PDFs and Zotero library?

Yes. Upload PDF, Word, Markdown or text files, or import from Zotero and Mendeley. Choose My Library as the source to ask across them.

CancellingCan I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account page and the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. The Defence Pass is one payment and never renews.
