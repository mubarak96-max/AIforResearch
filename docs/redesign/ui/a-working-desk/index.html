Summary

Sleep in the hours after learning improves later recall of declarative material compared with an equal period awake 12. The effect is linked to slow-wave sleep and hippocampal replay 23, and is larger for material learned with intent to remember 4. The idea is old: forgetting was shown to be slower across sleep than across waking in 1924 5.

What the evidence shows

14 of 18 studies report better recall after sleep than after the same time awake 13.

Sleep and memory consolidation: a review

Forgetting is slower across sleep than across waking, a finding first reported a century ago (Jenkins & Dallenbach, 1924). Later work ties the benefit to slow-wave sleep, when the hippocampus replays what was learned during the day (Rasch & Born, 2013). Reviews agree that the effect is strongest for declarative material (Diekelmann & Born, 2010).

Questions

Where do the sources come from?

From a scholarly index of 250M+ works (the OpenAlex catalogue), from PubMed for medical questions, from the web when you switch it on, and from the PDFs in your own Library. You choose the sources for each question.

Can it make up a citation?

Citations are taken from search results, not from the model's memory, so each one is a real record you can open. The sentences around a citation are still written by AI, so read the source before you rely on a claim.

Is it allowed at my university?

Rules differ by university, course and journal, so check yours first. AI for Research shows where every claim comes from and keeps an authorship report of what you wrote and what was suggested, so you can declare your use honestly.

What do I get for free?

30 credits a month: six Deep reports, or thirty Summary answers. Paper search, the citation generator, autocomplete and the Library do not use credits, and every workspace opens with a preview.

What is a credit?

Credits pay for the heavy work. A Summary answer is 1, a Deep report 5, a Detailed report 20, a document review 10 and a slide deck 10. Credits reset each month, and a top-up of 500 is $7.

Can I use my own PDFs and Zotero library?

Yes. Upload PDF, Word, Markdown or text files, or import from Zotero and Mendeley. Answers that come from your own files show the page they came from.

Can I cancel any time?
