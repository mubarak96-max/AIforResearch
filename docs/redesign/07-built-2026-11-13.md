## Data Lab statistics

**New tests** (`lib/statsMore.ts`):

- Spearman correlation.
- Wilcoxon signed-rank (exact below 50 pairs).
- Kruskal-Wallis.
- Fisher's exact test (2 × 2, with odds ratio).
- McNemar (exact).
- Logistic regression: numeric and categorical predictors, odds ratios with Wald CIs, the likelihood-ratio test and Nagelkerke R², with warnings for separation and events per variable.
- Kaplan-Meier with the log-rank test, plus a survival chart.
- Diagnostic accuracy: sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, likelihood ratios and exact CIs.
- ROC: AUC with the DeLong CI and the Youden cut-off, plus a ROC chart.
- Cronbach's alpha: item-total r and alpha if each item is deleted.

**Checked against references:** scipy 1.18, numpy, and published examples (Fisher's tea-tasting table, the 6-MP leukaemia Kaplan-Meier data, R's Wilcoxon example). See `tests/server/data-lab-stats.test.ts`.

**Test list:** `lib/dataLabAnalyses.ts` defines each test as data (its columns and settings). Saved analyses now take 1 to 41 columns and optional settings such as "positive", "event" and "has the condition". The calculation version is `data-lab-2026-11-13`; analyses saved under `data-lab-2026-11-12` still rerun.

**Sample size:** adds prevalence (Cochran, with finite population correction and design effect), correlation (Fisher's z) and diagnostic accuracy (Buderer).

**Code export:** `lib/dataLabCode.ts` writes R, Python and SPSS scripts from the saved specifications.

**"Which test?"** now covers diagnostic, survival and reliability goals, and points to the tests that are now in Data Lab.

**Scale scoring:** `lib/surveyScoring.ts` adds score and band columns, logged as a `derive` step in the dataset change log.

## Reporting-guideline check (Reviewer)

- **Checklists:** `lib/reportingGuidelines.ts` holds paraphrased items for CONSORT 2010, STROBE, PRISMA 2020, CARE, STARD 2015, COREQ, SRQR and SQUIRE 2.0, numbered as published. The CONSORT entry notes that CONSORT 2025 exists.
- **How the check works** (`lib/server/lib/guidelineCheck.ts`): items are sent in batches of 12, together with the text.
  - A "reported" verdict stands only when its quote is found in the text; otherwise the item becomes "check by hand".
  - Items the model skips are also marked "check by hand".
  - If every batch fails, the credits are refunded.
- **Route:** `POST /api/reviewer/guidelines`, at `CREDIT_COSTS.guideline_check = 5` per 10,000 words (at most 40,000 are read). The price is in the pricing table and its sync test.
- **History:** saved as task kind `guideline_check`. The UI suggests a guideline from the manuscript's wording and exports a CSV with a page-number column.

## Deliverable templates (Writing Studio)

- **Six modules**, under a "Deliverables" heading in the Writing Studio:
  - Case report (CARE)
  - Clinical audit (plan or report)
  - Evaluation report (OECD-DAC, evaluation questions or logframe)
  - Policy brief
  - Annotated bibliography
  - Seminar reading prep
- **Prompts** forbid inventing clinical details, results or data and leave bracketed placeholders instead. Case reports are written de-identified.
- **Blank editor templates** were added for five of them (all except reading prep). They don't appear in "Fill a template", because each has its own module.

## Survey builder

- **Pages:** `/surveys` (list), `/surveys/[id]` (builder) and the public form at `/s/[publicId]`. The form uses no account and no analytics, is marked noindex, rate-limited and has a honeypot.
- **API:** `/api/surveys` (signed in) and `/api/public/surveys`. Data is stored in `surveys/{id}` with a `responses` subcollection; responses store no identity. Account deletion now removes a person's surveys.
- **Question types:** single and several choice, Likert, number, short and long text. Each question has a variable code and an editable answer code for each choice.
- **Validated scales** (`lib/surveyInstruments.ts`): PHQ-9, GAD-7, WHO-5, PSS-10, RSES and SUS, with their published wording, scoring, bands, licence notes and citations. The PHQ-9 item 9 safety warning is shown in the builder.
- **Data integrity:** once a survey has responses, removing questions or answer codes is refused; wording changes and new questions are allowed. Saves are checked against the revision, so a stale save is refused.
- **AI:** "Draft questions" (3 credits) and "Check the questionnaire" (2 credits).
- **Exports:** the questionnaire as an editor document (Word or PDF), a codebook, a data-entry template and the survey file.
- **Plan limits:** Free gets 3 surveys and 100 responses each; paid plans get 5,000 responses per survey.
- **Data Lab:** `/data-lab?survey=<id>` loads the responses, adds the scale scores, and marks scored items as numeric.
